Terms of use

Effective 1 October 2026

Olwen is a personal project operated by Alex (alxsongs). Its private assistant is available only to the authorised owner and any person the owner explicitly permits to access the installation. This website provides information about that assistant and does not offer public registration.

Personal assistance

Olwen helps with research, notes, calendars and reminders. AI answers can be incorrect or incomplete. Check important facts against their sources and review dates, times and requested changes. Reminders depend on the server, connected services, network access and device notification settings, so delivery is not guaranteed.

Connected accounts

Connect only accounts you are authorised to use, review the requested permissions, and give clear instructions for changes. The assistant can act on connected accounts within those permissions. Google account access can be withdrawn at any time through Google Account connections.

Privacy

The Olwen privacy policy explains the information used by the assistant, local storage and notification services, and how to disconnect or remove saved information.

Availability and support

This personal installation can be changed, paused or retired by its owner. For support, use the user support email shown on Olwen's Google authorisation screen. Updates to these terms will be published here with a revised effective date.
