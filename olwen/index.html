Olwen is a private AI assistant for finding information, keeping notes and staying on top of the calendar.

Maintained by Alex for personal use, Olwen runs through a private assistant interface using locally hosted models. This is its public information page; access to the assistant is limited to its authorised owner.

01 / FIND

Research with sources

Search the web, read useful pages and bring their sources into a conversation. Answers can be checked against the original information.

02 / REMEMBER

Work with notes

Find, read, create and update notes in the owner's existing notes app, keeping useful information in one place.

03 / PLAN

Keep up with the day

Read Google Calendar, create or edit events when requested, and receive upcoming-event reminders in the assistant and on the owner's phone.

Connecting Google Calendar

The owner signs in with Google and chooses whether to grant calendar access. Olwen uses that access to answer scheduling questions, manage requested events and prepare reminders. Permission can be withdrawn through the Google Account at any time.

Calendar content used in a conversation is processed by the local assistant and may be saved in its history. Phone reminders send event details through the configured notification service. The privacy policy explains what is accessed, where it is stored and how to disconnect.

Contact and support

Olwen is operated by Alex (alxsongs). For support or a privacy request, use the user support email shown on Olwen's Google authorisation screen. This installation is for personal use and has no public registration.
